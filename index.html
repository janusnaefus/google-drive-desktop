A personal Google Drive connection using rclone, maintained by janusnaefus.

What this connection does

It makes the owner's Google Drive available as a folder in the Ubuntu file manager. After the owner signs in with Google, rclone can list and open files, and upload, rename, or delete them when the owner requests those actions.

The connection runs on the owner's computer and communicates with Google Drive. It is configured for personal use, not offered as a hosted service or an application for public sign-up.

Your data stays out of this website

This is an informational website. It does not provide sign-in, receive Google authorization tokens, or access Google Drive files. Read the privacy policy for details about the desktop connection, local storage, and this site's hosting.
